History admin · everyone

Past seasons are fetched once and used for track stats and the circuit pages. The default is 2000; you can go back as far as 1950.

Going further back means a longer first download. Pitwall fetches in batches of about 250 requests an hour (half of what the Jolpica API allows, so live results always get through), newest season first. 2000 onwards takes about an hour; going back to 1950 can take several hours. It starts within a minute of saving, picks up where it left off after a restart or an outage, and never fetches a finished season twice.
